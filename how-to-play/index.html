How to play

MX Royale is easy to start and hard to master. Press Ride now, pick a track and go – the game runs in your browser on PC, Mac, phone or tablet. This page explains the controls for keyboard, phone and controller, and how whips, scrubs and the gate start work.

Ride now

Keyboard

W / SThrottle / brake
A / DSteer · in the air: whip the bike
← / →Lean the bike · on the ramp: scrub · in the air: lay it flat
↑ / ↓Weight forward / back · stoppie / wheelie
SpaceCrouch / pop · in freeride: tricks together with the arrow keys
ShiftRear brake
RRespawn on the track
C · Q · G · EscCamera · look back (hold) · ghost on/off · pause

You can change every riding key in Settings.

Phone and tablet

  • Turn the phone to landscape.
  • Left thumb: a stick appears where you touch. Left/right steers (and whips in the air), up/down moves your weight.
  • Right thumb: hold GAS, brake sits to its left. Drag left/right on the throttle to lean the bike (scrub), drag down to load the suspension and let go at the lip to pop.
  • Top right: camera, respawn, pause. Prefer tilting? Switch on tilt steering under Settings → Phone steering.
  • On the Beginner and Normal levels the rider pops off jumps for you.

Controller (gamepad)

  • RT throttle, LT brake (both analog).
  • Left stick steers and whips in the air.
  • Right stick ↕ moves your weight, ↔ leans the bike (scrub, lay it flat).
  • A crouch/pop · X rear brake · Y respawn · Start pause.
  • Tricks in freeride: hold LB or RB in the air and use the left stick; LB + RB = no hands.

How to whip

A whip throws the bike sideways in the air. Steer in just before the lip (A/D, left stick or the touch stick), hold it in the air and let go about half a second before landing so the bike comes back straight. The better the timing, the further the whip goes – past 180° if you get it right. A whip counts from 40°.

How to scrub

A scrub keeps you low over a jump so you are back on the ground sooner, which is faster in a race. Lay the bike down on the face of the ramp (←/→, right stick, or drag on the throttle) and stand it back up before the end of the lip – otherwise the front wheel hooks.

Pop and gate start

Pop: hold crouch on the ramp and release it at the lip to jump higher and further. Gate start: from the “5” board hold throttle and brake together to build revs, then release the brake when the gate drops. Throttle without brake before that is a jump start.

Tricks and the trick school

In freeride and in the trick school you can do backflips, Supermans, Nac-Nacs, heel clickers, Can-Cans, seat grabs and cliffhangers. The trick school teaches each one step by step for keyboard, phone and controller, and tells you what went wrong.

Tips for faster laps

  • Land on the downslope behind a jump to keep your speed.
  • Cutting does not pay: leave the track and the lap does not count.
  • Stuck? Respawn puts you back on the course.
  • Start with Sunvale MX Park, the beginner track, and switch the rider aids (Beginner, Normal, Sport, Pro, Sim) under Settings when you get faster.
  • In time trial your own best lap rides along as a ghost – race it until you beat the medal times.
  • Four cameras: dynamic chase, classic chase, drone and helmet.
