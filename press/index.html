200 wordsMX Royale is a free motocross game that runs right in the browser. It works on PC, Mac, phones and tablets, with keyboard, touch controls or a gamepad, and needs no download and no account. There are 15 race tracks and 2 freeride maps: two supercross tracks under stadium lights, a beginner MX park, forest Grand Prix tracks, a desert jump park with a rail gap, a red-rock canyon, a snow track down a ski slope, a rainforest hollow, a beach at low tide, a quarry and a salt lake. Riders race up to 11 AI opponents, ride time trial against their own ghost for medal times and a top-10 leaderboard, take on the Daily Challenge or a four-race Championship. Online races work with a five-character room code; bots fill up the field. The riding is arcade, but with real technique: whips, scrubs, the pop off the lip and a proper gate start. In freeride there are backflips, Supermans, Nac-Nacs and Can-Cans, and a trick school explains each one. The game is available in English, German, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese. MX Royale is built by three students from Austria who ride motocross themselves. It has been in open beta since 26 November 2026, with no ads and no in-game purchases.
