The tracks

15 race tracks from a beginner MX park to supercross under lights, plus 2 freeride maps for tricks. New tracks are added during the beta.

  • Sunvale MX Park

    BeginnerMotocross

    The beginner track: a farm-country MX park with rollers, small tables and a creek ford.

  • Larkmeadow SX

    MediumSupercross

    Supercross in a night stadium: rhythm lanes, whoops, a sand section and a finish-line triple.

  • Frostline MX

    MediumSnow

    Snow track down a ski slope, along the chairlift and over the Frozen Creek Gap.

  • Monsoon Terraces

    MediumMotocross

    A flowing track over rice terraces on a hillside, with a terrace chain and a wooden aqueduct.

  • Mirage Flats

    Medium–hardDesert

    Desert jump park on a dry lake bed, with a rail gap over the train tracks and a long washboard.

  • Glutwald MX

    Medium–hardForest

    Autumn forest in evening light, with fire at three jumps, a big step-up and a big step-down.

  • Rustleaf Hollow

    Medium–hardForest

    A twisty autumn woods track with roots, a muddy hollow and a log-skidding lane.

  • Neon Harbor SX

    Medium–hardSupercross

    Supercross at night in a harbour hall: container table, crane turn, dock whoops and a triple.

  • Pine Ridge GP

    HardGrand Prix

    A long forest Grand Prix up a ridge, with Horsepower Hill, the Stump Double and the Ridge Double.

  • Sandglass Canyon GP

    HardCanyon

    Desert canyon Grand Prix: rock steps, a mesa table, a slot canyon, a cliff drop and a road gap.

  • Highcrest Valley GP

    HardMountain

  • Canopy Hollow

    HardRainforest

  • Tidewater Flats

    HardBeach

  • Ironcut Quarry

    HardQuarry

    A disused quarry: switchbacks up the mining terraces, a pit drop and a dragon’s back on the rim.

  • Saltpan Speedway

    HardSalt lake

    FreerideFreeride

Questions & answers

Is MX Royale free?

Yes, MX Royale is completely free to play. There are no ads and no in-game purchases.

Do I need to download or install MX Royale?

No, MX Royale runs directly in the web browser with no download and no install. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and no account is needed to start riding.

Can I play MX Royale on my phone?

Yes, MX Royale works on phones and tablets in landscape mode. It has touch controls with a thumb stick, throttle and brake buttons, and optional tilt steering.

Does MX Royale support a controller?

Yes, MX Royale supports gamepads. Throttle and brake are on the analog triggers (RT/LT), the left stick steers and whips, and the right stick moves your weight and leans the bike.

Can I race my friends online in MX Royale?

Yes, MX Royale has online races with room codes. One player creates a room and shares the 5-character code, the others enter it and the track loads automatically; bots can fill up the field.

How many tracks does MX Royale have?

MX Royale has 15 race tracks and 2 freeride maps. They include two supercross tracks and outdoor tracks in forest, desert, canyon, snow, rainforest, beach, quarry and salt-lake settings.

Is there supercross in MX Royale?

Yes, MX Royale has two supercross tracks: Larkmeadow SX in a night stadium and Neon Harbor SX in a harbour hall. Both have rhythm sections, whoops and triples.

Which game modes does MX Royale have?

MX Royale has races against AI riders, time trial with your own ghost, a Daily Challenge, a Championship of four races, freeride, a trick school and online races with friends.

Which languages is MX Royale available in?

MX Royale is available in English, German, Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese.
