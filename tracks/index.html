Motocross tracks

MX Royale has 15 race tracks and 2 freeride maps, all free to ride in your browser. They range from a beginner MX park to two supercross tracks and long outdoor Grand Prix tracks in forest, desert, snow and on the coast.

Sunvale MX Park

BeginnerMotocross

Most riders start here. Wide grassy turns, rollers and small tables lead over cow pastures and past hay bales, and one section runs straight through a creek. A good place to learn the pop and your first whips.

Race field: 10 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Larkmeadow SX

MediumSupercross

A short, tight supercross track under stadium lights. Rhythm lanes, a whoops section and a sand section come one after another, and the lap ends with an SX double and a triple over the finish line.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Frostline MX

MediumSnow

Frostline runs down a ski slope in winter: along the chairlift line, past snow cannons and over the Frozen Creek Gap. Snow rollers and a big jump into the finish straight close the lap.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Monsoon Terraces

MediumMotocross

A flowing layout on a terraced hillside. A chain of jumps drops from terrace to terrace, the track passes bamboo and runs along a wooden aqueduct. Rewards a smooth line more than raw speed.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Mirage Flats

Medium–hardDesert

A jump park in the desert. You clear the railway with the Rail Gap, hammer through a washboard section on the dry lake bed and finish with the Mirage table. Lots of air time, little room for mistakes on the landings.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Glutwald MX

Medium–hardForest

An autumn forest track in low evening light. Three jumps have fire next to them, the climb has a big step-up and the descent a big step-down into the valley. Leaves, roots and rhythm sections in between.

Race field: 10 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Rustleaf Hollow

Medium–hardForest

Narrow and twisty: Rustleaf winds through autumn woods over root carpets, through a muddy hollow, along a log-skidding lane and past an excavator. A track for precise steering rather than flat-out speed.

Race field: 10 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Neon Harbor SX

Medium–hardSupercross

The second supercross track sits in a harbour hall at night. You jump a table built from shipping containers, turn under a crane, fight through the dock whoops and go for the Harbor Triple.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Pine Ridge GP

HardGrand Prix

A classic outdoor Grand Prix through pine forest. The track climbs Horsepower Hill, runs along the ridge over roots and doubles and comes back down through the woods. Long laps, a full gate of 12 riders.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Sandglass Canyon GP

HardCanyon

Sandglass takes you through red desert canyons. Rock steps and slabs, a table on top of a mesa, a narrow slot canyon, a drop off a cliff and a Road Gap over the highway, then back down the wash through the whoops.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Highcrest Valley GP

HardMountain

The fastest outdoor track: Highcrest climbs 38 metres through forest and rock, over limestone slabs and up switchbacks to the summit, then drops through a rock gate and past a waterfall.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Canopy Hollow

HardRainforest

A twisting rainforest track down in a hollow. Jump a canyon gap, cross a suspension bridge, ride through a tunnel of trees and past a waterfall, and keep your balance on the dragon’s back.

Race field: 10 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Tidewater Flats

HardBeach

Sand riding on the coast at low tide. Ripples and soft sand everywhere, a gap over a tidal creek, a turn by the lighthouse, a lane between the groynes and a line straight through an old shipwreck.

Race field: 10 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Ironcut Quarry

HardQuarry

Ironcut climbs a disused quarry in switchbacks from terrace to terrace. On top you ride a dragon’s back along the rim before a steep drop back down into the pit.

Race field: 10 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Saltpan Speedway

HardSalt lake

Wide open and fast: Saltpan crosses a salt lake with long straights and rollers. The highlights are the Salt Crust Triple and a table next to the salt works’ conveyor belt.

Race field: 12 riders · Time trial with ghost and medals

Dust Devil Freeride

FreerideFreeride

No laps, no timer, no opponents. Dust Devil is an open map with jump lines, a step-up, a hip and a foam pit to practise tricks without consequences.

No laps, no timer, no opponents

Midnight Arena

FreerideFreestyle

A freestyle motocross show at night in a stadium. Separate ramps for whips, backflips, Nac-Nacs, Can-Cans and big air, plus a quarterpipe and fireworks.

No laps, no timer, no opponents
