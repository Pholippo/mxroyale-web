Privacy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. The German version is the legally binding one.

In short: MX Royale has no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools. We only store what the game needs to work. An account is optional.

Controller

Philipp Grabner, Techelsberg am Wörthersee, Austria · privacy@mxroyale.com

This website

mxroyale.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically required data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties; fonts are served from this website.

The game

The game runs on a server at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. Connections go through Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., USA), which processes technically required connection data (e.g. IP address).

  • Without an account: settings, best times and ghost laps stay in your browser (local storage). We receive nothing unless you submit a lap to the leaderboard.
  • Online races: your chosen rider name and your position are sent live to the other riders in the room. Nothing is stored after the race. Chat messages are only relayed, not stored; only if someone is reported do we keep their last 10 messages (with name, without IP) for review.
  • Leaderboards and ghosts: for a leaderboard time we store rider name, lap time, track, the ride data of the lap (so others can race your ghost) and the riding aids used.
  • Account (optional): username, password (only as a secure hash, never in plain text), optionally an email address (for password reset and sign-in links) or a passkey. Signing in sets a technically required session cookie. You can delete your account yourself in the game at any time.
  • Clips: shared replay clips are public via their link and are deleted automatically after 14 days; you can delete them earlier yourself.
  • Bug reports: if you send a bug report, we store your text, a screenshot and technical details (game version, browser, graphics card). We do not store your IP address, only a non-reversible hash against abuse.
  • Abuse protection: to rate-limit requests the server keeps IP addresses briefly in memory without storing them permanently.

Email

If you write to an @mxroyale.com address, we process your message to reply. Our email provider is Purelymail (USA).

Legal bases

Game, account, leaderboards and clips: providing the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Security, abuse protection and bug reports: legitimate interest in a safe, working game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to providers in the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Children

You can play without an account. If you are under 14 (or the age of digital consent in your country), you need your parents' consent to create an account.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to privacy@mxroyale.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).
