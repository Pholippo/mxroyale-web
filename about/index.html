MX Royale fact sheet

Last updated: 27 November 2026

What is MX Royale?

MX Royale is a free motocross racing game that runs in the web browser. It needs no download and no account, and it works on PC, Mac, phones and tablets with keyboard, touch controls or a gamepad.

What can you do in MX Royale?

In MX Royale you race motocross and supercross bikes on 15 race tracks and 2 freeride maps. Game modes are races against up to 11 AI riders, time trial against your own ghost with medal times, a Daily Challenge, a Championship of four races, freeride, a trick school and online races with friends via a 5-character room code.

Which tracks are there?

MX Royale has two supercross tracks (Larkmeadow SX, Neon Harbor SX) and outdoor tracks in forest, desert, canyon, snow, rainforest, beach, quarry and salt-lake settings. The beginner track is Sunvale MX Park.

Who makes MX Royale?

MX Royale is developed by three students from Austria as a hobby project. The game has been in open beta since 26 November 2026; it has no ads and no in-game purchases.
